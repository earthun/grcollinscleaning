Our Story

Since 2009

As the US economy struggled to recover from the 2008 recession, Geoffrey Collins began offering personalized cleaning services to budget-conscious, owner-operated office and retail sites throughout San Antonio and the surrounding areas.

As the economy slowly recovered, Geoffrey developed a knack for building cleaning and facility maintenance plans specifically designed around each client's needs and budget.

Over the years, the size of our company — and the size of the facilities we clean — has changed dramatically. However, no matter the size, we clean each and every facility with the same passion and attention to detail as we did at our very first job.
