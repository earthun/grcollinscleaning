Your walkthrough, step by step

Every job starts the same way, whether it's a small office suite or a multi-tenant retail space — we look before we clean.

  1. We walk the space together

    You show us the areas that matter most — high traffic zones, sensitive equipment, problem spots.

  2. We build a custom scope

  3. You review and approve

    No pressure. Ask questions, request changes, and start whenever you're ready.

  4. We get to work

    Consistent, detail-oriented service from the same team that walked your space with you.
